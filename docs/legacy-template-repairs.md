# Legacy template repairs

The compiler now preserves all 106 reports in `legacy-layouts.json` alongside the twelve original shared profiles. `legacy-catalog.json` describes the supported brand/language/mode combinations. Source definitions remain unchanged; narrow compatibility adjustments are documented in `scripts/legacy-layout-adjustments.mjs`.

The resolver chooses the exact source variant for built-in families. A brand's `legacyBrand` is its source token (for example `METEORA`, `SUFRO`, or `PAPADOPOULOS`); FAETHON's existing key `1` remains supported. Unknown numeric brand identities are not guessed. Administrators can enter the source token in the brand editor. Custom user geometry remains authoritative and validated profiles are not silently replaced during seeding. A newly selected variant does not inherit physical validation from another variant.

Eight template choices are added to the existing fourteen: small/large no-logo custom labels and six reference lists. The existing blank-sample and A4 butcher choices now use the recovered report geometry. Lists use the actual imported data and paginate; blank reference descriptions stay blank rather than inventing their translations. Their table presentation is adapted for legibility, not claimed pixel-identical to Access.

Access fields for pieces, condition, production text, customer address/contact and butcher titles are mapped. Ionic customer code/origin and Zlaths packaging date/comment have explicit production inputs. Missing values remain blocking warnings. The exact recovered brand artwork is preserved rather than overwritten with the generic FAETHON logo.

Text measurement uses enlarged unhinted metrics before conversion back to millimetres. Measuring glyph bounds directly at a 2–3 logical-pixel font rounds bounds enough to discard dates and narrow captions, even when the final PDF has ample resolution. A source-specific adjustment widens the Zlaths LOT field, two translated captions, and gives the Italian expiry caption two lines. Conditional labels follow the associated animal/slaughterhouse/freeze field.

PDF rendering paginates reference tables and certificates. The PNG preview displays page one; preview responses include `pageCount` and `sourceReport`. The single-raster printer transport rejects multi-page documents instead of silently submitting only page one; these must be printed through the PDF.

The certificate follow-up restores Access `BreakHeader` / `BreakFooter` and `FormHeader` / `FormFooter` sections and correctly trims the `NotDefault` visibility flag. `CertificateLayouts` composes section-relative coordinates on A4, preserving the exported letterhead, declarations and approval images. Conformance restores the missing recipient/company block, uses five readable table rows per page with continuous numbering, and prints imported Intrastat codes. Bulgarian certificates honor the explicit page break: each product/lot receives its own two-page pair, with that product's recipe ingredients, dates, cartons and weight. This prevents different products from sharing ambiguous date columns. The supplied BG PDF's four pages for one product included unwanted spillover; fresh and frozen products now intentionally produce two complete pairs. Narrow cells and the overprinted packing statement are reflowed. Source declarations are reproduced, not updated or independently certified.

Uploaded brand logos now override the logo slot in recovered label layouts, and custom-label headings use the selected brand's name. Unmatched private brands do not inherit FAETHON's QR link. Fixed manufacturer approval artwork remains part of the underlying label layout.

`scripts/qa-certificate-private-label.mjs` generates live PDFs with two fictional brands, customer details and uploaded test logos. Fixtures are archived in a `finally` block; original products are not edited. `scripts/qa-certificate-audit.py` checks pages, lot coverage and private-label names, and creates a compact combined review PDF. Generated PDF/image/ZIP files under `output` are ignored. Previously tracked QA binaries are removed only from the Git index and remain on disk; existing Git history is not rewritten.

## Layout fidelity follow-up (23 September 2026)

The compiler now preserves weight, italic/underline, legacy borders and stroke widths, fixed numeric precision, image sizing/DPI, growth/shrink flags, and control-identity visibility rules extracted from the report events. The original Access definitions remain untouched. Generated JSON omits default-valued metadata to keep changes reviewable.

The renderer distinguishes the automatically seeded `LOGO 4` hash from uploaded logos, preserving the horizontal artwork in all sixteen small FAETHON variants. Other uploaded hashes still override the logo slot. Backed captions that overlap images paint last so the large logo cannot obscure the ingredients caption; other source stacking is preserved. Thick footer dividers have a small explicit clearance adjustment; the Italian expiry/weight repair now fits 80 mm. Nutrition headers use the actual energy/RI column centers instead of space-count alignment.

`LabelText` shapes, measures and draws at one enlarged logical size, with stable font baselines. It preserves bold, italic, underline, paragraphs, line breaks and matching imported ingredient runs. Stale runs do not overwrite subsequently edited ingredients. Distributed alignment is applied to wrapped lines. Source font weights are requested from the installed family; Calibri remains preferred. Bundled Carlito Italic and BoldItalic cover missing italic faces (this Mac has Calibri regular/bold but its italic request resolves upright). Normal fields can fit metric differences down to 85%; single-line nutrition heading cells can fit down to 70%. Larger overflow is reported and clipped to the field rather than silently dropping the entire line. Growth is bounded by the next overlapping field and the physical page; fixed label stock does not gain extra pages or overlap downstream content. Shrink reduces the field box without rearranging the rest of the physical template.

Brands now have editable `names` by language. The existing FAETHON record receives Greek `ΦΑΕΘΩΝ` and English `FAETHON` only where those entries are absent, using the recovered heading and wordmark. Existing entered translations are preserved. Other missing translations are reported; the renderer does not repeat a Greek name as an invented translation. Additional source brand-name translations still require master-data entry because those database values were not present in the report exports.

The standard unvalidated `thermal-small` and `zebra-small` profiles return to the documented 100×80 mm stock. Validated profiles are preserved. Other template sizes and the intentional certificate/reference-list composition and generated Code 39 implementation remain as previously documented.

Verification: 38 backend tests passed, including a new render regression over all 98 non-certificate/non-list report variants with rich ingredients; 3 private-workbook tests were skipped because the paired source workbooks were unavailable in their configured directory. The existing certificate pagination/render tests passed. All 10 browser tests and the frontend production build passed. An additional local render pass used the archived imported pork-product/recipe data across all 98 label variants and reported no render issues. The small/large Greek and Greek-English PDFs were inspected at 200 dpi. Local review files are under `output/pdf/layout-fidelity/`. No physical print or barcode scan was performed.

## Ingredient legends and footer rules (24 September 2026)

The annotated screenshot exposed a stacking defect after restoring Access's opaque category backgrounds: source control order painted the category backplate after the ingredient body. Layout compilation now identifies ingredient frames structurally, centers their short category/caption legends on the frame edge, and assigns explicit paint layers. Rendering centers the actual single-line glyph bounds, rather than assuming the font ascent fills the control. Ingredient bodies paint after those legends and begin below their boxes, preserving the original bottom boundary. These adjustments apply to 63 matching source variants and to the corresponding shared profiles; layouts without this frame structure retain their geometry. Full-width small-label categories remain on their separate line.

The earlier attempt to remove the instruction-bottom borders was rejected after comparison with the real legacy label. Both that removal and the older upward divider shift are now removed. All instruction boxes retain closed borders; the thick horizontal rule retains its exported position and weight (GR/EN: Top=4665 twips, BorderWidth=2 points). Footer text that starts at the rule's top is inset below its stroke instead of moving the rule. This restores the single heavy lower edge without the separated duplicate rule.

The reference export places the center line at 4215 twips, the ingredient seam at 4222, and instruction edges at 4221/4223. Matching vertical lines and instruction edges within 0.15 mm now join the shared ingredient seam; rule heights and vertical positions are unchanged. The original definitions remain untouched. This is a narrowly bounded join correction, not a replacement layout.

Validation: 45 tests passed, 3 private-workbook tests skipped. Coverage includes all 98 label variants, measured legend ink centering, a pixel check that late opaque headings cannot cover the ingredient layer, closed-panel border pixels, and a direct read of Access line definitions across all reports to verify rule visibility, position, dimensions and weight (with only the bounded vertical-join exception). The printed reference product and the user's gyros product 3-100-2-041 were regenerated through Resolver without render issues. Large GR/EN and small GR output was visually inspected. Updated review files are under `output/pdf/seeded-legacy-label/`; `gyros-review.png` corresponds to the user's latest screenshot. No physical print verification was performed.

## Instruction padding and cross-template audit (24 September 2026)

Instruction text now has a 0.7 mm left/right and 0.4 mm top/bottom inset in 63 report variants, including small labels, large labels, cartons and the sample label. The renderer wraps, measures, fits and clips within that content area while drawing the original outer border/background. The sample's instruction control extends 0.406 mm below its separate OptionGroup frame in the export; its bottom inset accounts for that difference. Its first footer row also clears that frame's lower stroke. Source frame and line geometry is preserved.

Two additional defects were reproduced and corrected:

- METEORA RO/EN large and carton labels escaped the earlier Greek/English caption filter, allowing their logo to overlap the Romanian ingredient heading. Header clearance now identifies frame legends by geometry, independent of language.
- DEIGMA_ETIKETA_GR exports the barcode after LOT, unlike the other small labels. Its opaque background erased the upper portion of the LOT text. LOT and its caption now paint after that background, with barcode dimensions and LOT coordinates retained.

Validation: 47 tests passed; 3 private-workbook tests skipped. New pixel checks cover rich-text padding without border movement and the sample's late barcode background. Header-clearance checks now cover all matching report legends. All 98 label variants were rendered with populated instruction text, and representative large, small, carton, Romanian, sample, pallet, custom and butcher layouts were visually reviewed. Gyros and the supplied real-label product were regenerated through Resolver with their actual seeded Greek/English instructions and no rendering issues.
